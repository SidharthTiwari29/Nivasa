# Nivasa Phase 1 — Step 2 Completion Gates

## Purpose

Step 2 completes only when market intelligence becomes production-grade infrastructure for the frozen Nivasa journey. A source list alone is not completion.

## Mandatory pipeline

Source discovery → governance → compliant acquisition → raw evidence → normalization → canonical product → source SKU/variant → price observation → geography/unit/tax semantics → historical observations → cross-source matching → alternatives → confidence/freshness → provenance → user-facing explanation.

## Coverage requirements

The source universe must cover the complete Indian interior ecosystem, including:

- interior design platforms and designers
- manufacturers and authorized dealers
- marketplaces and specialist retailers
- local vendors, fabricators, installers and artisans
- furniture and storage
- kitchens and wardrobes
- hardware and fittings
- plywood, MDF, HDHMR, laminates and veneer
- stone, quartz, marble, granite and solid surfaces
- tiles and flooring
- paints, wallpapers and wall finishes
- ceilings and acoustic systems
- sanitaryware, faucets, bath and plumbing
- electrical, switches, wiring and smart-home devices
- lighting and fans
- appliances
- doors, windows, glass and architectural fittings
- curtains, blinds, rugs, carpets and furnishings
- mattresses and soft goods
- décor, mirrors, clocks, planters and accessories
- art, paintings, murals, sculptures and Indian crafts
- mandir/pooja products, accessories and specialist artisans
- outdoor, balcony and garden products
- services, labour, logistics and installation

## Data integrity

Every product observation must distinguish, where applicable:

- MRP/list price
- selling price
- dealer/quote price
- indicative market rate
- material rate by unit
- labour/fabrication
- installation
- logistics/delivery
- tax/GST treatment
- geography
- availability
- observation timestamp

No estimate may be presented as a verified price. No warranty, specification, corrosion-resistance claim, material claim, availability claim or product identity may be invented.

## Product evidence

A product record must support, where available:

- real product image/evidence
- brand
- exact product/model/SKU
- dimensions
- material
- finish
- features
- compatible accessories
- warranty evidence
- source URL/evidence
- freshness timestamp
- confidence
- alternative products
- why Nivasa recommends it
- why an alternative may be better/worse

## Architecture alignment

Step 2 must feed the frozen product journey:

Home Intelligence → Home DNA → Budget Reality → Design → Product Intelligence → What-If/Savings → Smart Home → Buildability/BOQ → Shop Your Home → Visualization → Walkthrough → Execution.

No Step 2 feature may introduce a competing product direction.

## Engineering gate

Every production domain follows:

model → service → API → authorization → tests → CI → integration → merge

## Completion definition

Step 2 is not complete merely because CI is green, documentation exists, or a source registry contains 500 names. Completion requires executable persistence, governed acquisition, representative real-source adapters, normalization, SKU identity, price observations, provenance, matching, alternatives, tests, monitoring/replay and user-facing transparency foundations.

## Explicit quality bar

The system must support the smallest meaningful interior decision as well as large-ticket decisions: a hinge, handle, switch, bell, diya, painting, curtain accessory, mirror, light fitting or drawer organizer must be representable with the same provenance and price-integrity principles as a sofa, wardrobe, kitchen or sanitaryware package.
