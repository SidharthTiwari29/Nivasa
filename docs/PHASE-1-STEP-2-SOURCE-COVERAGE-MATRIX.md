## Expansion rule

The matrix is not a whitelist. Research continues until at least 500 distinct sources are registered and category/geography gaps are reviewed. Regional/local and specialist sources are first-class sources. A source can be registered with manual, partner, licensed or user/vendor-provided acquisition when automated extraction is not permitted.

## Quality rule

No product or price is considered market truth merely because a source is listed. Product-level observations require provenance, timestamp and source evidence; estimates and recommendations remain explicitly separate.
